## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Keep it honest** - These are for you, not for anyone else. Write what actually happened.

2. **Batch on Friday EOD** - 15 minutes on Friday captures the week while fresh. Don't skip.

3. **Update INDEX immediately** - After monthly retro, update REFLECTIONS_INDEX.md. Makes patterns visible.

4. **Reference decisions** - When reflecting, link to related decisions you made earlier. Build your own knowledge graph.

5. **Look for patterns** - After 3 months, read all weeklies + monthlies at once. Patterns emerge.

6. **Teach from reflections** - Share insights with team. Makes them more real and helps others.

## 🚨 Common Pitfalls (Avoid These)

❌ **"I'll write it all at once at month-end"**
- Weekly rhythm is key. Friday EOD = fresh data. Month-end is too late.

❌ **"I'll make it really detailed and thorough"**
- No. 15 minutes max per week. Quality matters, but speed matters more. Done beats perfect.

❌ **"I'll skip a week because I was too busy"**
- Especially then! Busy weeks are most important to capture. Takes 15 min, reveals patterns.

❌ **"I won't look at old reflections"**
- At each monthly/quarterly, read all prior ones. Patterns hide until you look.

❌ **"I'll keep all this private"**
- Some insights should stay private, but sharing learnings with team amplifies impact.
